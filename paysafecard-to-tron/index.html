Energy, bandwidth and why TRX is needed

Every Tron transaction consumes bandwidth, and token transfers such as USDT also consume energy. If your wallet has none, the network burns TRX from your balance to cover it. If it has no TRX at all, the transfer simply fails. Holding a modest TRX balance solves that, and staking TRX in a wallet such as TronLink gives you a daily allowance of energy that can make USDT transfers almost free.

Quick checks

How much TRX is enough?

Burning TRX for a single USDT transfer to a new address costs a few TRX at current fee levels, and less if the recipient has received USDT before. If you send USDT often, staking a larger amount pays off. For an occasional transfer, a small balance kept unspent in the wallet is enough.

Tron questions

Is TRX the same as TRC-20 USDT?

No. TRX is Tron's own coin; USDT on Tron is a token that runs on the network and needs TRX for fees.

Can I get USDT and TRX in one order?

One order pays out one asset. Place a second order, or buy USDT and swap a small part to TRX on an exchange.

What happens if the address doesn't start with T?

Stop. It belongs to another network, and TRX sent there won't arrive.

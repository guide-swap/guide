Addresses and subaddresses

A Monero primary address starts with 4; subaddresses start with 8. Both are long, about 95 characters. Wallets let you create as many subaddresses as you like, all feeding the same balance. Using a fresh subaddress for each purchase keeps incoming payments unlinkable to each other, which is good practice.

Because transactions are private, your wallet has to scan the blockchain to find payments meant for you. If a newly opened wallet shows zero, let it finish syncing before worrying.

The 10-block lock

Monero makes newly received coins unspendable for 10 blocks, about 20 minutes. It's a protocol rule that protects privacy and guards against chain reorganisations, not a hold placed by anyone. Your wallet will show the balance as locked until then.

Exchanges and Monero

Several large exchanges have delisted XMR in some regions. Check that your exchange still supports Monero deposits before using its address; a self-custody wallet avoids the question entirely.

Monero questions

Can I see the payment on a block explorer?

Not in the usual way. Monero explorers don't show amounts or addresses; your wallet is the place to confirm receipt.

Is holding Monero legal?

In most countries, yes. A few restrict exchanges from listing it. Check the rules where you live.

My wallet shows 0 after the payment. Why?

It's probably still syncing. Let it scan to the current block height and the payment will appear.

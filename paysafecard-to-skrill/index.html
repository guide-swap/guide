Same group, separate products

Paysafecard and Skrill are both owned by Paysafe, yet they are run as different products with different rules. Depending on your country, Skrill may offer Paysafecard as a deposit method with its own fee, or not offer it at all. Where it isn't available, or where you'd rather receive a known amount without a deposit fee, an exchange is the alternative.

The money arrives in Skrill as a peer-to-peer payment to your email address. From there it behaves like any other Skrill balance: spend it online, send it to other Skrill users or move it on.

Avoid these mix-ups

  • Use the email Skrill shows under Settings, not an old address.
  • Choose the currency your Skrill wallet is held in.
  • Make sure your Skrill account can receive payments; some restricted accounts can't.

Using the money for gaming and betting

Many people load Skrill because a game store, poker room or betting site accepts it. Once the payment is in your Skrill balance it can be used wherever Skrill is accepted, subject to the merchant's own rules and your local laws. Bear in mind that some sites restrict bonuses for Skrill deposits; check their terms before you play.

Skrill questions

Can I receive GBP in Skrill?

Skrill payouts are available in EUR and USD. If your Skrill wallet is in GBP, choose EUR or USD and Skrill will hold or convert it according to its own rules.

Do I need a verified Skrill account?

Receiving usually works on unverified accounts within Skrill's limits. If Skrill asks you to verify before releasing funds, follow its instructions in the app.

Is it faster than depositing Paysafecard inside Skrill?

It is comparable in speed, and the amount you receive is fixed in the exchange form before you pay.

What if I typed the Skrill email wrong?

If the address doesn't belong to a Skrill account, the payment can't be completed. Contact exchange support straight away with your order number so it can be corrected.

Your wallet, your coins

The payout lands at the address you give, so who controls that address controls the coins. A self-custody wallet such as a hardware device or an app where you hold the recovery phrase puts you in charge. An exchange address works too, but then the exchange holds the BTC for you.

Bitcoin addresses come in a few formats: bc1q… (SegWit), bc1p… (Taproot), 3… and the older 1…. All of them can receive. Copy the address from your wallet rather than typing it, and compare the first and last few characters after pasting.

  • Mobile: BlueWallet, Sparrow (desktop), Muun.

Why Bitcoin takes longer than the payment

The exchange sends the transaction quickly, but the Bitcoin network adds blocks about every ten minutes, and wallets wait for one or more confirmations. On a busy day with a crowded mempool that can stretch from minutes to an hour or more. A pending transaction is normal; check its status on mempool.space using your address.

Bitcoin questions

How is the BTC amount calculated?

From the live rate when you request the payment link. The rate is then fixed for your order.

Can I buy a fraction of a Bitcoin?

Yes, any amount. A typical voucher buys a fraction of one BTC, measured in satoshis.

Can I send the BTC to an exchange?

Yes, use the exchange's Bitcoin deposit address on the Bitcoin network, not on BNB Chain or another chain.

Is a Lightning invoice accepted?

No, payouts are on-chain only. Use a regular Bitcoin address.

How an exchange runs, start to finish

  1. Describe what you holdSay whether you have a voucher with a 16-digit PIN or money sitting in a myPaysafe account, enter the PIN or the account email, then choose the country and currency it belongs to.
  2. Pick the destinationChoose where the money should go and fill in the one detail that destination needs: an email, an IBAN, a card number or a wallet address.
  3. Get your payment linkLeave an email address and request the link. The rate is fixed at this moment, so the figure you saw is the figure you get.
  4. Pay on Paysafecard's own pageThe link opens the official Paysafecard checkout, where you confirm the payment with the voucher code or your myPaysafe login.
  5. Receive the payoutOnce the payment clears, the converted amount is sent to the destination you gave. Most orders finish within 5 to 10 minutes.

Why Paysafecard can't simply be moved

Paysafecard was built to pay online without a bank card. That design is also its limit: a voucher has no account number, so PayPal, Revolut or a bank cannot pull money from it. When plans change, or a voucher was a gift you can't use, the only practical way to move the value is to sell it to an exchange service that pays you in the method you prefer.

The pages on this site describe that route for each common destination, so you know in advance which details to have ready and what a realistic arrival time looks like.

What to check before you pay

  • The receive currency. Match it to the currency of your account or card. Letting PayPal, a bank or a wallet convert it again usually costs more than choosing it correctly up front.
  • The payout detail. Copy email addresses, IBANs and wallet addresses instead of typing them. A wrong character is the most common reason a payout stalls.
  • The network, for crypto. USDT on Tron and USDT on Ethereum are not interchangeable. The wallet or exchange receiving it must support the network you select.
  • The final amount. The form shows exactly what will arrive. If it doesn't match your expectations, stop before paying.

Questions people ask first

Can I exchange a myPaysafe balance, or only vouchers?

Both. Choose account balance in the first step and enter the email of your myPaysafe account instead of a PIN. You confirm the payment by logging in on the official Paysafecard page.

Which Paysafecard currencies are accepted?

Vouchers and balances in EUR, GBP, USD, PLN, CAD, AUD, CHF, CZK, DKK, HUF, NOK, SEK, RON, TRY, MXN, AED, SAR and KWD. The payout can be in a different currency; the form shows the converted figure before you confirm.

How long does an exchange take?

Most orders complete within 5 to 10 minutes of payment, at any hour. Crypto payouts also depend on network confirmations, and bank transfers on the receiving bank.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The exchange works per order: you leave an email for the payment link and the payout details for the destination, and nothing else.

Is there a minimum amount?

Yes, the smallest order is 20 EUR or the equivalent in another currency, for example around 100 PLN.

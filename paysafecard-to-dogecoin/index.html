What people do with DOGE

Some hold it as a speculative bet, some tip creators or friends, and a growing list of shops and services accept it at checkout. Its low fees make it practical for small amounts, which is also why it pairs well with a single voucher rather than a large purchase.

Unlike Bitcoin, Dogecoin has no supply cap: about five billion new coins are created every year. That keeps inflation steady and the price driven largely by demand.

Exchanges list DOGE on two chains

Some exchanges offer a Dogecoin deposit on BNB Chain as well as on the Dogecoin network. The payout is on the native Dogecoin network, so pick that option on the deposit page; its address starts with D, while a BNB Chain address starts with 0x.

Dogecoin questions

How fast are Dogecoin transfers?

A new block arrives roughly every minute, so a few confirmations take only minutes.

Can I buy DOGE with a GBP voucher?

Yes. Choose GBP as the voucher currency; the DOGE amount reflects the converted value.

Why is my exchange showing a different address format?

You may have picked the BNB Chain option. Switch the deposit network to Dogecoin.

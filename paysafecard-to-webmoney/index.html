Purses, not accounts

A WebMoney account, the WMID, can hold several purses, one per currency. Each purse has its own number: a Z purse for dollars, an E purse for euros, followed by twelve digits. A payout must go to the purse whose currency matches the one you select, otherwise it can't be credited.

Paste the purse number from your WebMoney Keeper. Don't use your WMID; it identifies you, not a purse.

Which purse?

  • WMZ if you pay or trade in dollars.
  • WME if your partners and shops work in euros.
  • Create the purse in Keeper first if you don't have it yet.

From voucher to WebMoney purse

  1. Choose the Paysafecard sourceVoucher PIN or myPaysafe account balance.
  2. Enter voucher detailsPIN or email, country, currency and amount.
  3. Select WMZ or WMEThis decides which of your purses gets the money.
  4. Paste the purse numberZ… for WMZ or E… for WME, plus your contact email.
  5. Pay and refresh KeeperAfter the Paysafecard payment, the purse balance updates.

Verification and limits

WebMoney ties limits to your passport level. The basic alias level is enough to receive moderate amounts, while larger or regular transfers may need a higher level. If an incoming payment is refused for a limit reason, raising your level in WebMoney usually solves it.

WebMoney questions

What is the difference between WMZ and WME?

WMZ is denominated in US dollars and WME in euros. Pick the one you intend to spend or hold.

Can a GBP voucher go to a WMZ purse?

Yes. The form converts GBP to USD and shows the amount the purse will receive.

I entered my WMID instead of the purse. What now?

Contact exchange support with the order number before paying, or as soon as you notice, so the purse can be corrected.

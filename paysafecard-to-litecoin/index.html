Why people choose LTC

A new Litecoin block arrives roughly every two and a half minutes, and a typical transfer costs well under a cent. Exchanges usually credit LTC deposits after a handful of confirmations, which in practice means minutes rather than the half hour or more Bitcoin can take. Many people use LTC simply as transport: they receive it, then sell or swap it on an exchange.

Address formats

  • ltc1… is the modern SegWit format.
  • L… and M… are older formats, still valid.
  • Don't use a Bitcoin address; they look similar but belong to another network.

Privacy option in your wallet

Litecoin's MWEB extension lets you move coins into a confidential balance where amounts aren't public. Wallets such as Litecoin Core and Cake Wallet support it. The payout arrives at your normal address; you can opt into MWEB afterwards from the wallet.

Litecoin questions

How many confirmations do I need?

For a personal wallet, one is enough to see the coins. Exchanges set their own number, usually a few.

Can I convert a euro voucher to LTC?

Yes. The rate for EUR to LTC is shown and fixed when you request the link.

Is the minimum the same as for other coins?

Yes, 20 EUR or the equivalent.

Buy Gram, formerly Toncoin, with Paysafecard

Same network, new name

Only the coin's name and ticker changed, from Toncoin (TON) to Gram (GRAM). Wallets, addresses and the network itself stayed the same, so a Tonkeeper or Telegram Wallet address you already have keeps working. Some exchanges and apps may still show the old name for a while.

TON addresses come in two common forms: user-friendly ones starting with UQ or EQ, and a raw form starting with 0:. Paste whatever your wallet shows.

Wallets

The memo for exchange deposits

Like XRP tags, exchanges often use one TON address for all users and identify your deposit by a memo or comment. Sending without it means the coins reach the exchange but not your account. Telegram's custodial Wallet usually doesn't need one; self-custody wallets never do.

Using GRAM in Telegram

Inside Telegram you can buy Stars for paid content and mini-app purchases, send GRAM to contacts in a chat and pay in shops built as mini-apps. A small balance in TON Space is enough to get started.

Gram questions

Do I need to swap old Toncoin for Gram?

No. The rename didn't require any migration; existing coins simply carry the new name.

Can I receive GRAM straight into Telegram?

Yes, to Wallet in Telegram or TON Space. Copy the receive address from the app.

Is this the same as USDT on TON?

No. This page is for GRAM. For USDT on TON, choose USDT and the TON network.

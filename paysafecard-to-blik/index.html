Cash out Paysafecard to a Polish bank with BLIK

Why BLIK

BLIK is built into almost every Polish banking app, from PKO BP and Pekao to mBank, ING and Santander. Transfers through it settle in seconds and run around the clock, weekends and public holidays included, which makes it the fastest way to receive złoty without waiting for a session-based bank transfer.

Most people use this route for a voucher bought for a purchase that never happened, or a gift card they would rather have as money in the account.

Good to know

  • PLN vouchers from Poland are the natural fit, but EUR or other vouchers can be converted to PLN.
  • Phone transfers through BLIK must be enabled in your banking app.
  • Turn on notifications to see the credit the moment it arrives.

Paysafecard to BLIK in five steps

  1. Choose voucher or myPaysafeMost Polish vouchers are printed receipts with a 16-digit PIN.
  2. Enter the PIN, Poland and PLNThen the amount you want to cash out.
  3. Select BLIK as the payoutThe receiving currency is złoty.
  4. Fill in the BLIK detailsUsually the phone number registered for BLIK transfers, as described in the form, plus your email.
  5. Pay through the linkConfirm the voucher on the Paysafecard page; your bank app shows the incoming BLIK transfer.

Vouchers bought in Poland

Paysafecard in Poland comes in fixed PLN denominations. You don't have to exchange the whole value: enter the amount you want and the rest stays on the PIN. Keep the receipt until the exchange is complete; the PIN printed on it is the only proof of the voucher.

BLIK questions

Do I need a Polish bank account?

Yes. BLIK only works with Polish banks, so the payout goes to an account held at one of them.

Can I exchange a euro voucher to BLIK?

Yes. Select EUR as the voucher currency and BLIK as the payout; the złoty amount is shown before you confirm.

Does it work at night or on Sundays?

Yes. BLIK runs 24/7 and so does the exchange, so payouts aren't held until the next banking day.

What is the minimum for BLIK?

The smallest order is about 100 PLN, the equivalent of 20 EUR.

Paysafecard as income

It's not unusual for small online jobs, especially in gaming communities, to be paid in Paysafecard. That's convenient for the payer and awkward for the person earning it, because a PIN can't be invoiced, withdrawn or shown to a tax adviser. Moving it into Payoneer gives you a normal account balance with a record of the incoming payment.

Payoneer then lets you withdraw to a local bank account, pay suppliers or spend with its card in many countries.

Good to know

  • Use the email you log in to Payoneer with.
  • Your Payoneer account should be approved and active.
  • Choose the currency of the balance you withdraw from most.

Keeping records of the payment

The confirmation email you receive for each order lists the amount, the currency and the date. Keep it with your Payoneer statement. If you declare freelance income, it documents where that part of your balance came from.

Payoneer questions

Which Payoneer currencies are supported?

EUR, GBP and USD.

Do I need receiving accounts enabled in Payoneer?

No. The payment goes to your Payoneer email, so local receiving account details are not required for this route.

How long until I can withdraw?

As soon as the balance appears in Payoneer, usually minutes after payment. Payoneer's own withdrawal times then apply.

Can my client pay me directly through this?

The voucher holder places the order. If a client gives you a PIN, you place the order yourself and enter your Payoneer email as the payout.

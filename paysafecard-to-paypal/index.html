Why PayPal and Paysafecard don't connect

PayPal funds an account from a bank, a card or an incoming payment. A Paysafecard voucher is none of these: it is a prepaid code with no owner name and no account behind it, so there is nothing PayPal can link or debit. In most countries PayPal has never offered Paysafecard as a top-up method, and the few regional experiments have long ended.

What PayPal does accept, from anyone, is a payment to your email address. That is what an exchange produces. The exchange service receives your Paysafecard payment and sends the equivalent amount to your PayPal account, where it shows up like any other money you've been sent and can be spent, held or withdrawn.

Before you start

  • Log in to PayPal and note which currency your balance is held in.
  • Use the exact email address your PayPal account is registered with, not an alias.
  • If your account is new, finish PayPal's verification so incoming money isn't put on hold.

  1. Tell the form what you're paying withPick voucher if you have a 16-digit PIN, or account balance if the money is in myPaysafe.

Choosing the right PayPal currency

PayPal accounts are multi-currency, but each has a primary currency. If you receive USD into a EUR account, PayPal either holds the dollars as a separate balance or converts them with its own margin when you spend. Picking the receive currency that matches your primary balance avoids that second conversion entirely.

A practical rule: euro-area accounts take EUR, UK accounts GBP, US accounts USD and Canadian accounts CAD. If you're unsure, PayPal shows your primary currency under Wallet in the app.

When a PayPal payment is held

New or unverified PayPal accounts sometimes place incoming money on hold or ask the owner to confirm their identity before releasing it. That's a PayPal account rule, not a problem with the exchange, and it usually clears once you complete the requested steps in PayPal. Verifying your account before the first exchange saves the wait.

PayPal questions

Will the money appear as a balance or a pending payment?

As a received payment that adds to your PayPal balance. Unless PayPal applies one of its own holds, it is available straight away.

Can I combine two vouchers into one PayPal payout?

Each exchange order covers one voucher or one myPaysafe payment. For several vouchers, place one order per voucher, or load them into myPaysafe first and exchange the balance in one go.

Can I send the money to someone else's PayPal?

Yes. The payout goes to whatever PayPal email you enter, so double-check it, because a payment sent to a wrong but existing address cannot be pulled back.

Are there fees on top of the shown amount?

No. The amount displayed in the form is what is sent to PayPal. PayPal itself may charge you later if you convert currencies or withdraw to a bank.

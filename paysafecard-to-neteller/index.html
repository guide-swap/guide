When Neteller is the better destination

Neteller's appeal lies in being accepted by many gaming and trading platforms that don't take PayPal. If that's where you plan to use the money, sending it straight to Neteller saves you a second transfer later. It also keeps the funds separate from your everyday bank or card.

The exchange pays your Neteller account the same way another Neteller user would. You don't have to link anything or change any setting in your wallet.

Where to find your account ID

  • Web: open Settings, then Account details.
  • App: tap the menu; the 12-digit number sits under your name.
  • Email works too, if it's the one Neteller has on file.

What happens to the money afterwards

Inside Neteller you can pay merchants, transfer to other users, withdraw to a bank account or spend with Neteller's card where it's offered. Each of those has its own Neteller fee, so it's worth looking at Neteller's fee page if you plan to move the money on rather than spend it within the wallet.

Neteller questions

Which currencies can Neteller receive here?

EUR and USD. Choose the one your Neteller account uses to avoid a conversion inside the wallet.

Is there a fee for the exchange?

There is no separate charge; the rate already includes the service margin, and the form shows the exact amount Neteller will receive.

Can I use the funds on betting sites?

Yes, wherever Neteller is accepted, within the site's terms and your local regulations.

Does Neteller need to be verified?

Receiving normally works, but Neteller may ask you to verify before you can withdraw or move larger sums.

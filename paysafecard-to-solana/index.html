SOL as the entry ticket

On Solana, every transaction and every new token account costs a small amount of SOL. If you want to buy tokens on Jupiter, mint an NFT or receive USDC on Solana in a fresh wallet, you need a starting SOL balance first. A voucher covers that with room to spare.

Solana addresses look like a long mix of letters and numbers without the 0x prefix. They're case-sensitive, so always copy and paste.

Keep a little SOL behind

Solana accounts must hold a minimum balance, called rent, to stay active, and each token you hold uses a small deposit of SOL. If you swap all your SOL into a token, you may not be able to move that token later. Leaving a small amount unspent avoids the problem.

Solana questions

Can I stake the SOL I receive?

Yes. Wallets like Phantom and Solflare let you delegate SOL to a validator directly in the app.

Is this USDC on Solana?

No, this page is for SOL. For USDC on Solana, choose USDC and the Solana network.

How do I verify the transfer?

Search your address on solscan.io or in your wallet's activity tab.

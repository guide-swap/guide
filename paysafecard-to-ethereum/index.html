The 0x address and what it can hold

An Ethereum address begins with 0x followed by 40 characters. The same address can hold ETH and every ERC-20 token, which is why it's also the address you'd use for ERC-20 USDT later. The payout is made on Ethereum mainnet. Layer-2 networks such as Arbitrum or Base use the same address format, but they're separate networks, and a mainnet payout doesn't appear there.

Buying ETH with Paysafecard

  1. Choose voucher or myPaysafeThe first option in the exchange form.
  2. Enter the Paysafecard detailsVoucher code or account login email, then country and currency; the ETH figure updates as you type the amount.
  3. Pick EthereumThe ETH equivalent is shown right away.
  4. Paste your 0x addressThen your contact email for the payment link.
  5. Pay and look it up on EtherscanAfter payment, search your address on etherscan.io to watch the transfer.

Starter uses for a small ETH balance

A few euros of ETH cover the network fees for many token transfers, and a larger amount opens up decentralised exchanges, staking services and NFT marketplaces. If you plan to use cheaper layer-2 networks, you can bridge part of the ETH after it arrives.

Ethereum questions

Is the ETH sent on mainnet or a layer-2?

On Ethereum mainnet.

Can I receive to a MetaMask wallet?

Yes. Copy the account address from MetaMask and make sure the Ethereum Mainnet network is selected when you check the balance.

Why do I see the transaction on Etherscan but not in my wallet?

The wallet may be showing a different network or account. Switch to Ethereum Mainnet and the balance appears.

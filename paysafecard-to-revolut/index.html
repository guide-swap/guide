Send Paysafecard money to your Revolut account

Revolut accepts cash top-ups in a few countries, but not Paysafecard codes. Exchange the voucher instead and choose how Revolut receives it: to your card, your IBAN or your @Revtag.

Three ways to receive into Revolut

Revolut gives every account several public identifiers, and an exchange can pay to any of them. Which one you choose changes little about the amount, but it can change the speed and how the payment appears in your app.

  • Card number. The most common choice. The money is credited to the card's account balance and shows as an incoming transfer.
  • IBAN. A standard bank transfer to your Revolut account. Good for larger sums and for EUR, where SEPA payments usually land quickly.
  • @Revtag. Revolut's username system. Handy if you'd rather not share card digits, as long as the Revtag is spelled exactly.

Find your details in the app

  • Card number: Cards, select the card, then show details.
  • IBAN: Home, tap the currency balance, then Details.
  • Revtag: tap your profile picture; it starts with @.

Pick the balance you'll spend from

Revolut holds separate balances per currency and converts between them at its own rate, which on some plans and at weekends includes a markup. If you mostly spend in pounds, receiving GBP is cheaper than receiving euros and converting later. A GBP voucher can still be paid out in EUR or USD; the form shows the converted amount before you confirm.

Is it safe to share these details?

An IBAN and a Revtag exist so that people can send you money; nobody can withdraw funds with them. A card number alone can't be charged without the expiry date and security code, which the exchange never asks for. Keep those two to yourself and you've given away nothing that works against you.

Revolut questions

Do I need Revolut Premium or Metal?

No. Any Revolut account can receive payments, including Standard. Higher plans only change Revolut's own fees and limits.

Can a UK voucher be paid out in euros?

Yes. Choose EUR as the Revolut currency; the voucher's GBP value is converted and the result is shown before you pay.

How fast is it compared to a normal top-up?

Card and Revtag payouts usually show within minutes of the Paysafecard payment. IBAN transfers are also quick in EUR, but can take longer in other currencies depending on the payment rail.

What's the smallest amount I can send to Revolut?

Orders start at 20 EUR or the equivalent in your voucher's currency.

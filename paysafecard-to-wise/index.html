Prepaid Bridge / Paysafecard to Wise

Fund your Wise account with a Paysafecard voucher

Wise is built for receiving money from anywhere, just not from prepaid codes. An exchange bridges the two: the voucher is paid on Paysafecard's site and the value is sent to your Wise balance.

Who uses this route

Wise is popular with freelancers, students abroad and anyone who holds several currencies. If part of your income or a gift arrives as Paysafecard, Wise is a sensible place to park it: you can hold it, convert it at Wise's mid-market based rates, send it on or spend it with the Wise card.

The exchange pays into Wise the same way a client or an employer would. Wise doesn't need to know where the money came from in advance, but like any regulated account, it may ask about the source of larger or unusual incoming payments.

Email or IBAN?

  • Email is enough when the Wise account is in your name and active.
  • IBAN suits EUR payouts and makes the transfer look like a regular bank payment.
  • For GBP, your Wise account number and sort code are in the GBP balance details.

How to exchange Paysafecard to Wise

  1. Choose voucher or myPaysafeSelect the type of Paysafecard money you are exchanging.
  2. Describe the voucherEnter its PIN or the myPaysafe email, the country it comes from, its currency and the amount.
  3. Select Wise in the right currencyPick EUR, GBP or USD to match the Wise balance you want to grow.
  4. Enter your Wise detailsAdd your Wise email or IBAN, then an email address for the payment link.
  5. Pay and watch the balanceFinish the payment on the Paysafecard page; the amount appears in Wise shortly after.

Wise and Revolut for this purpose

Both work well as a destination. Wise tends to suit people who move money between countries or keep savings in several currencies, while Revolut is built more around everyday card spending. If you already have one, use it; there is no advantage in opening the other just for a Paysafecard exchange.

If Wise asks about the payment

Wise sometimes asks account holders to explain an incoming transfer, especially the first time money arrives from a new sender. A short, honest answer such as "proceeds from selling a prepaid voucher" plus the order confirmation email is normally all that's needed.

Wise questions

Can I receive into a Wise Business account?

Yes. Use the business account's email or IBAN in the payout field. The name on the account doesn't need to match the person who bought the voucher.

Can I receive GBP from a euro voucher?

Yes. Choose GBP as the payout currency and the form converts the voucher amount, showing the result before payment.

Why is my Wise balance still empty after an hour?

Check that the email or IBAN was entered exactly, then search Wise for a pending incoming payment. If nothing shows, contact exchange support with your order number.

Does Wise charge to receive the money?

Receiving in EUR and GBP to your account details is normally free. Some USD transfers carry a small Wise receiving fee, which Wise lists in its pricing.

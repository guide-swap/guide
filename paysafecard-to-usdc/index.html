USDC or USDT?

Both aim to stay at one dollar. USDC is issued by a US company under US and EU rules and publishes monthly attestations of its reserves; under the EU's MiCA rules it can be offered on European exchanges where USDT has been restricted. USDT has deeper liquidity on Asian exchanges and on Tron. If your exchange or app is European, USDC is often the smoother choice.

Network check

  • Ethereum (ERC-20): the most widely supported, higher fees to move.
  • Solana: native USDC, very cheap and fast.
  • Polygon: native USDC, low fees.
  • BNB Chain: cheap, mainly for Binance users.

Watch for bridged versions

Some wallets show both native USDC and older bridged tokens such as USDC.e. On Ethereum, Solana and Polygon the payout is native USDC issued by Circle; on BNB Chain, USDC is the Binance-bridged version. If your wallet lists two USDC tokens, add or select the right one, otherwise the balance may look like zero even though the coins arrived.

USDC questions

Is USDC the same as a dollar in the bank?

It's designed to be redeemable one-to-one, but it isn't a bank deposit and isn't covered by deposit insurance.

Can I send USDC to Coinbase or Kraken?

Yes. Choose USDC and the matching network on their deposit page, then paste that address.

Do I need ETH to receive ERC-20 USDC?

Not to receive it. You'll need a little ETH later to send it from a self-custody wallet.

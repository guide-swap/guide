Withdraw Paysafecard money to your bank account

How fast a bank transfer is

The exchange sends the transfer within minutes of your payment. When it lands depends on the payment rail. In the euro area many banks receive SEPA Instant transfers in seconds; standard SEPA takes until the next banking day. UK accounts receiving pounds usually get Faster Payments, which arrive almost immediately. International USD transfers are the slowest and can take one to two banking days.

What to enter

  • EUR: your IBAN and the account holder's name.
  • GBP: account number and sort code, or a UK IBAN.
  • USD: IBAN or account number plus the bank's SWIFT/BIC.

Getting the name right

Banks increasingly check that the account name matches the IBAN or account number, especially in the UK with Confirmation of Payee and in the euro area with the new verification of payee checks. Enter the name exactly as it appears on your statement. A mismatch can delay or bounce the transfer.

Bank transfer questions

Which countries are supported?

Euro-area and EEA accounts via SEPA, UK accounts in pounds, and international accounts for USD transfers.

Can I send to an account that isn't mine?

Yes, enter the recipient's details and name. Make sure they're correct, because a completed bank transfer can't be reversed by the sender.

Will my bank ask where the money came from?

Usually not for small amounts. If it does, the order confirmation email explains the source of the payment.

Is a bank transfer slower than PayPal or Revolut?

Often slightly, because banks process transfers in their own cycles. For speed, a Revolut or Wise account receives the same money faster.
